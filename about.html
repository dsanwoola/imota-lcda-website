Who We Are

Imota Local Community Development Area

Imota LCDA is one of the Local Community Development Areas in Ikorodu Federal Constituency, Lagos State, Nigeria. Created to bring governance closer to the people, Imota LCDA serves a vibrant and growing community of over 120,000 residents.

We are committed to providing efficient public services, transparent governance, and sustainable development across all communities within our jurisdiction.

The LCDA operates under the Lagos State Local Government Service Commission and is guided by the Lagos State House of Assembly's legislative frameworks for community development areas.
