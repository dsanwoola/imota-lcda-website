Executive Chairman's Message

Building a Better Imota — Together

Dear People of Imota,

It is with deep humility and immense gratitude that I address you as the Executive Chairman of Imota Local Community Development Area. The trust you have placed in my leadership and that of this administration is something I hold with the highest sense of responsibility.

When we assumed office, we made a solemn promise to the people of Imota — a promise of transparency, inclusivity, and tangible development. Today, I am proud to report that we are keeping that promise, one project at a time, one community at a time.

"Our commitment is to build a prosperous, safe, and inclusive Imota — where every resident has access to quality infrastructure, responsive governance, and opportunities to thrive."

We have embarked on road rehabilitation, school renovation, solar street lighting, borehole provision, and primary healthcare improvement — projects that directly touch the lives of our people. We are equally focused on economic empowerment through skills acquisition programmes and partnerships that create opportunities for our youth and women.

Good governance is not just about building roads and schools. It is about ensuring that every voice is heard, every complaint is addressed, and every naira is accounted for. This is the standard we hold ourselves to, and we welcome you to hold us accountable to it.

This website is part of our commitment to open governance — a platform where you can access services, track our projects, read our announcements, and communicate with your council. We believe an informed community is a stronger community.

I encourage every resident of Imota to participate actively in our town hall meetings, report issues that affect your community, and take advantage of the services and programmes your LCDA provides. This is your government, and it is here to serve you.

Together, we will build the Imota we all deserve.

Hon. Sunday Benson

Executive Chairman, Imota LCDA

Ikorodu Federal Constituency, Lagos State
