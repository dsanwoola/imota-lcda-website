Accessibility Statement

Imota LCDA is committed to ensuring this website is accessible to all residents, including persons with disabilities.

♿

Our Commitment

Imota LCDA strives to ensure that this website is inclusive, accessible, and usable by all members of the public, regardless of disability or assistive technology used.

Conformance Status

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

Features We Have Implemented

Keyboard navigation support
Semantic HTML structure
Alt text on all images
Sufficient colour contrast
Responsive mobile design
Descriptive link text
Form labels and error messages
ARIA landmark roles
Readable font sizes
Skip-to-content links

Known Limitations

Some older PDF documents on this site may not be fully accessible. If you need information from a PDF in an accessible format, please contact us and we will provide an alternative.

Feedback & Contact

We welcome feedback on accessibility. If you experience any barriers, please contact us:
