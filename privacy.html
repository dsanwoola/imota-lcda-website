Privacy Policy

How Imota LCDA collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Imota LCDA is committed to protecting your privacy. This Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone, and address when submitting a form or registering for a service.
  • Service Information: Information required to process government service requests.
  • Usage Data: Anonymised data about how you interact with our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and respond to enquiries and service requests
  • To send official announcements and newsletters (with your consent)
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal and statutory obligations as a government body

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with Lagos State government bodies where required by law. All sharing complies with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

  • Access personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Withdraw consent for newsletter communications at any time

7. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy: info@imotaLCDA.gov.ng or visit the Contact page.
