Our Projects

Ongoing, completed, and planned development initiatives across communities in Imota LCDA.

OngoingInfrastructure

Imota–Agric Road Rehabilitation

Full reconstruction of 3.2 km arterial road connecting Imota to Agric junction. Includes drainage channels and pedestrian walkways.

65% Complete · Est. Completion: Sept 2026
OngoingEducation

Primary School Renovation – 4 Schools

Renovation of classrooms, roofing, toilets, and fencing at Imota Primary School I, II, III, and Alapere Primary School.

80% Complete · Est. Completion: July 2026
OngoingUtilities

Solar Street Lighting – Phase 2

Installation of 200 additional solar street lights across 8 communities following the success of Phase 1.

45% Complete · Est. Completion: Aug 2026
PlannedHealth

Primary Health Centre Upgrade

Equipping Imota PHC with modern diagnostic equipment, maternity ward expansion, and additional consulting rooms.

20% Complete · Est. Start: Q3 2026
PlannedEnvironment

Imota Market Waste Management System

Structured waste collection, sorting stations, and recycling facilities for the Imota main market and surrounding areas.

Planning Phase · Est. Start: Q4 2026
CompletedUtilities

Solar Street Lights – Phase 1 (120 Units)

Successful installation of 120 solar street lights across 6 communities including Imota Town, Alapere, and Ebute.

Completed: May 2026
CompletedCommunity

Imota Community Hall Renovation

Complete renovation of the LCDA Community Hall including new roofing, tiling, electrical works, and modern seating for 500 persons.

Completed: March 2026
CompletedWater

Borehole & Water Stations – 5 Communities

Solar-powered boreholes with water distribution stations, providing clean water access to over 15,000 residents.

Completed: January 2026
