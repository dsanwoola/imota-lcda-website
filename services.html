Citizen Services

Services & Information

Access government services offered by Imota LCDA — from vital registration to permits and social welfare.

📋

Births & Deaths Registry

Department of Civil Registration

The Births and Deaths Registry handles the registration of all vital events occurring within Imota LCDA. Registration is free and compulsory under Nigerian law. Certificates are issued within 5–10 working days.

Birth Certificate (0–90 days)
Late Birth Registration
Death Certificate
Marriage Registration
Certificate Replacement
Record Correction
Visit Registry Office
🏗️

Permits & Licences

Department of Physical Planning & Development

All building developments, business operations, and outdoor activities within Imota LCDA require appropriate permits. Applications are reviewed within 10–21 working days depending on complexity.

Building Permit
Business Premises Licence
Signage & Billboard Permit
Street Trading Permit
Event / Public Gathering Permit
Renovation Approval
Apply Now
💳

Revenue & Taxes

Department of Finance & Revenue

Imota LCDA collects statutory levies and rates that fund community development, infrastructure, and social services. All payments are receipted and transparent.

Ground Rent / Tenement Rates
Market Stall Fees
Business Levy
Slaughter Slab Fees
Refuse Collection Levy
Motor Park Levies
Pay Online / Visit Office
🤝

Social Welfare

Department of Social Development

The Social Welfare Department supports vulnerable members of our community including the elderly, persons with disabilities, youth, and low-income families.

Poverty Alleviation Grants
Youth Skills Training
Elderly Support Programme
Women Empowerment
Persons with Disabilities Support
Community Mediation
